SuggestionChip lowers the cost of answering. Offer two to four, always including an escape ("Not sure").

```jsx
<ChipRow>
  <SuggestionChip>Looking for something</SuggestionChip>
  <SuggestionChip>Just pacing</SuggestionChip>
  <SuggestionChip>Not sure</SuggestionChip>
</ChipRow>
```

They never replace the composer — the caregiver can always type instead.
