Sparkline shows the shape of a trajectory without inviting the caregiver to read exact values.

```jsx
<Sparkline values={[40, 52, 44, 60, 55, 70, 66]} />
```

Bars ramp from sage-100 (oldest) to sage-600 (this week). Keep `height` at 46 or more — the bars are percentage-based and collapse in a shorter box.
