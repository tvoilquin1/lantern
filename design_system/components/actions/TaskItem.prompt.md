TaskItem is an item in "what's in front of me". Two or three, never a backlog.

```jsx
<Card variant="dark">
  <SectionLabel onDark>In front of you</SectionLabel>
  <TaskItem onDark>Two hours of respite care this week — I found three options near you.</TaskItem>
</Card>
```

Write each so the companion has carried part of the load already.
