Card is the only surface container in Lantern — use it for every block of content on a screen.

```jsx
<Card variant="paper">
  <SectionLabel>Where Dad is</SectionLabel>
  <StageHeadline stage="Early" qualifier="moderate" />
</Card>
```

Variants: `paper` (default, raised), `muted` (flat, secondary weight), `dark` (the sage-900 block — at most ONE per screen, reserved for "what's in front of you"), `panel` (hairline ring, no lift; used inside modals and briefs).
