Lantern — MVP UI exploration — turn 1
Two calm directions for the Companion and the Dashboard
Both are mobile-first (web + PWA), both answer the dashboard's three questions, both keep the coaching boundary — no diagnosis language, clinical framing stays descriptive. Both open in the conversation — the dashboard is a tap away, never the greeting. 1a is warm and editorial: paper tones, soft contrast, one thing at a time. 1b is quiet and structured: cool neutrals, clearer data surfaces, more legible trajectory over time.
1a
Lamplight — warm, editorial, one thing at a time
Linen paper ground, sage as the only true accent, one sans throughout. Risk is spoken in words first and color second, so a yellow state never reads as an alarm.
